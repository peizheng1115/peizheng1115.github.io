## 1. Scientific Figure Drawing & Illustration

Creating clear, accurate, and visually compelling figures is critical for manuscript acceptance and conference communication.

### Illustration & Vector Graphics Tools
* **[BioRender](https://www.biorender.com/)**: Industry-standard platform featuring thousands of standardized, scientifically accurate biomedical icons (cells, receptors, organoids, microfluidic chips, labware).
* **[Inkscape](https://inkscape.org/)**: Free, open-source professional vector graphics editor (SVG standard). Excellent for assembling multi-panel figures, plotting axes, and custom microfluidic channel schematics.
* **[Adobe Illustrator](https://www.adobe.com/products/illustrator.html)**: Gold-standard professional vector editing software used by top design studios and scientific publishing offices.
* **[SciDraw](https://scidraw.io/)**: Free, community-driven repository of high-quality scientific drawings and vector icons created by scientists.
* **[Servier Medical Art (SMART)](https://smart.servier.com/)**: Over 3,000 free, medical-grade vector illustrations available under Creative Commons licenses.

### 3D Molecular & Structural Visualization
* **[PyMOL](https://pymol.org/)**: Premier molecular visualization system for rendering high-resolution proteins, nucleic acids, and small-molecule binding pockets.
* **[UCSF ChimeraX](https://www.cgl.ucsf.edu/chimerax/)**: Next-generation software for molecular structures, density maps from cryo-EM, and volumetric data.
* **[RCSB Protein Data Bank (PDB)](https://www.rcsb.org/)**: Worldwide repository of 3D macromolecular structures.

### Scientific Color Palettes & Accessibility
* **[ColorBrewer 2.0](https://colorbrewer2.org/)**: Diagnostic tool for selecting colorblind-safe, print-friendly categorical, sequential, and diverging color maps.
* **[Viridis & Cividis](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/viridis/vignettes/intro-to-viridis.html)**: Perceptually uniform colormaps that preserve contrast and information even when printed in grayscale or viewed by color-deficient readers.
* **[Adobe Color Wheel](https://color.adobe.com/create/color-wheel)**: Harmonic color scheme generator with built-in accessibility contrast checking.

### Journal Figure Preparation Guidelines
1. **Resolution**: Minimum 300 DPI for photographs/halftones; 600–1200 DPI for combination charts and line art.
2. **Typography**: Use clean, universal sans-serif fonts (Arial, Helvetica, or Calibri) with consistent point sizes across panels (typically 6–8 pt for labels, 8–10 pt for panel titles).
3. **Dimensions**: Design specifically for target column widths:
   * Single column: ~85–90 mm (3.3–3.5 inches)
   * 1.5 column: ~120–140 mm (4.7–5.5 inches)
   * Double column: ~170–180 mm (6.7–7.1 inches)

## 2. Academic English & Scientific Writing

Writing clear, precise, and concise academic English accelerates the peer-review process and broadens your paper's impact.

### Phrasebanks & Dictionaries
* **[The University of Manchester Academic Phrasebank](https://www.phrasebank.manchester.ac.uk/)**: Exceptional resource providing organized sentence starters and connectors categorized by manuscript section:
  * *Being Critical*: Framing limitations of previous studies constructively.
  * *Describing Trends & Methods*: Expressing increases, decreases, correlations, and experimental protocols.
  * *Explaining Results & Signifying Importance*: Highlighting novel contributions without over-claiming.
* **[WordReference](https://www.wordreference.com/)** & **[Cambridge Academic English Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/)**: Reliable references for academic collocations and prepositions.

### Grammar & Writing Assistants
* **[Writefull](https://www.writefull.com/)**: AI-powered language tool trained specifically on published academic papers and scientific corpora.
* **[Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com/)**: Real-time checking for clarity, passive/active voice balance, and conciseness.
* **[Hemingway Editor](https://hemingwayapp.com/)**: Highlights overly complex sentences, excessive passive voice, and readability scores.

### Manuscript Preparation & Reference Management
* **[Overleaf (Collaborative LaTeX)](https://www.overleaf.com/)**: Cloud-based LaTeX editor with official journal templates (IEEE, Springer, Elsevier, Nature).
* **[Zotero](https://www.zotero.org/)**: Free, open-source reference manager with browser extensions, PDF full-text indexing, and automatic BibTeX export.
* **[Mendeley](https://www.mendeley.com/)**: Citation management and academic networking tool.

## 3. Literature Search & Scientific Discovery

* **[PubMed / NCBI](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/)**: Premier biomedical search engine.
* **[Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/)**: Comprehensive open access biomedical literature and data links.
* **[Connected Papers](https://www.connectedpapers.com/)**: Visual graph tool to discover related papers, seminal works, and prior research based on co-citation analysis.
* **[Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org/)**: AI-powered academic search engine highlighting key takeaways and influential citations.
* **[bioRxiv & medRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/)**: Leading preprint servers for life sciences and medical sciences.


## Language & International Communication

* **[🇩🇪 Goethe-Zertifikat A2: Offizielle Wortliste & Memory-Guide](/german-a2/)**: A comprehensive German A2 vocabulary memory system (~1,300 words) categorized by part of speech (Verbs, Nouns by gender, Adjectives, Prepositions, Conjunctions) with [Interactive Audio Web App](/german-a2/app.html) and digital flashcards.
